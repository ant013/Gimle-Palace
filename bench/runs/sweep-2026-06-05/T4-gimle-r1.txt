## How `MoneroAdapter` gets its dependencies

The init signature in `Unstoppable/Unstoppable/Core/Adapters/MoneroAdapter.swift:35` is:

```swift
init(wallet: Wallet, restoreSettings: RestoreSettings, node: Node) throws
```

So `MoneroAdapter` itself takes **three explicit constructor-injected parameters**. The pattern is **manual constructor injection driven by a hand-written factory class (`AdapterFactory`), not a DI container** (no Swinject / no Resolver / no Needle is used). Specifically:

1. **`wallet: Wallet`** is forwarded from `AdapterFactory.adapter(wallet:)`'s argument (which is dispatched on `(wallet.account.type, wallet.token.blockchainType)`).
2. **`restoreSettings: RestoreSettings`** is resolved by calling `restoreSettingsManager.settings(accountId:blockchainType:)` on the `RestoreSettingsManager` that was constructor-injected into `AdapterFactory`.
3. **`node: Node`** is resolved by calling `moneroNodeManager.node(blockchainType: .monero).node` on the `MoneroNodeManager` that was constructor-injected into `AdapterFactory`.

`AdapterFactory` is itself constructed once in the composition root, `Unstoppable/Unstoppable/Core/Core.swift:318`, where all 13 of its collaborators (`EvmBlockchainManager`, `EvmSyncSourceManager`, `MoneroNodeManager`, `BtcBlockchainManager`, `TronKitManager`, `TonKitManager`, `StellarKitManager`, `ZanoKitManager`, `SolanaKitManager`, `RestoreSettingsManager`, `CoinManager`, `SpamWrapper`, `EvmLabelManager`) are passed in via its `init`. The factory is then handed to `AdapterManager` (which `AdapterFactory` conforms to via `IAdapterFactory`), and `AdapterManager` is what actually invokes `adapterFactory.adapter(wallet:)` per wallet.

In addition to the three constructor parameters, `MoneroAdapter.init` also pulls **two service-locator-style dependencies** from the `Core.shared` singleton inside its body (not via init):
- `Core.shared.logger.scoped(with: "MoneroKit")` — used as the `logger:` for `MoneroKit.Kit`.
- `Core.shared.reachabilityManager` — passed as the `reachabilityManager:` for `MoneroKit.Kit`.

So the full picture is: **constructor injection at the call site (`AdapterFactory`) for per-wallet collaborators, plus implicit access to two app-wide singletons (`logger`, `reachabilityManager`) via `Core.shared`.** There is no DI container — `Core` acts as a hand-rolled composition root that builds `AdapterFactory`, and `AdapterFactory` then does a `switch`-based factory dispatch that calls `MoneroAdapter.init` with managers it already holds.
