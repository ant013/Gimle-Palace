Five `@Test` methods cover:

1. `supportsUsdcOnEthereumMainnetWithEip55Address` — EIP-55 mixed-case input (the form quoted in the task).
2. `supportsUsdcOnEthereumMainnetWithLowercaseAddress` — canonical lowercase form actually stored.
3. `v1TokenQueriesContainsUsdcOnEthereumMainnetExactlyOnce` — idempotency: future regressions that double-add USDC mainnet would fail this.
4. `doesNotSupportUsdcAddressOnWrongChain` — chain-scoped negative case (the Ethereum USDC address is NOT the Base USDC address `0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913`).
5. `rejectsUnknownErc20OnEthereumMainnet` — WETH negative case to lock that "any ERC-20 contract on Ethereum" is NOT auto-whitelisted.
