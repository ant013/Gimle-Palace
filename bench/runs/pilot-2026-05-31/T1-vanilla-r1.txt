## Class

`MoneroAdapter` — adapts the Monero blockchain to the app's wallet/adapter interface. It wraps `MoneroKit.Kit` and conforms to the app's adapter protocols (`IAdapter`, `IBalanceAdapter`, `ITransactionsAdapter`, `IDepositAdapter`) plus `MoneroKitDelegate`.
